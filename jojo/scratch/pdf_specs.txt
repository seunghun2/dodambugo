### 2. 축문 (Prayer Text / Ritual Document) Specifications

#### Size: **210mm × 297mm** (A4 size) — for both 일반 (General) and 기독교 (Protestant)

#### 축문 Content Layout:
The prayer text is written in traditional **right-to-left vertical columns** on a 338×479px artboard area with a background image (traditional paper texture, 68% opacity).

**Traditional (한문/Hanja) Prayer Text Structure:**
```
유 세차 경자년 정월 신묘삭초나홀갑오일
(Year, month, day in traditional calendar format)

顯 叔 父 學 ... (Traditional honorary Chinese characters arranged in vertical columns)
```

**Modern (한글) Prayer Text Structure:**
```
유 세차 경자년
(Year designation)

삼가 산신령께 고 하나이다.
아버님의 유택을 이곳에 정했사오니
산신께서 보호하고 돌보아 주시어
훗날 어려운 일이 없도록 보살펴 주시기 바라옵니다.
삼가 맑긍 술과 포와 과일을 갖추어
산신께 공선히 올려 드리오니
흠향 하시옵소서.
```
(Translation: "We humbly report to the mountain spirit. As we have chosen this place for our father's resting ground, we pray the mountain spirit will protect and watch over it so there will be no difficulties in the future. We humbly offer clear wine, dried meat, and fruits to the mountain spirit. Please accept our offerings.")

**Date Format:** `2021년 2월 8일 000` (year/month/day + name placeholder)

### 5. Business Rules (Extracted from Design Patterns)

1. **Religion-based branching**: The entire feature branches based on religion type:
   - **일반 (General)** and **불교 (Buddhist)** share the same tablet design/size
   - **기독교 (Protestant)** and **천주교 (Catholic)** share a different design/size
   - This aligns with the existing `funeral-display` skill rules

2. **Prayer text versions**: Two parallel versions exist:
   - 한문 (Hanja/Chinese characters) — traditional
   - 한글 (Korean) — modern/simplified

3. **Required form fields** (inferred from templates):
   - Deceased name (고인 이름)
   - Religion type (종교)
   - Date (날짜) — both solar and lunar calendar
   - Relationship to deceased (관계 - e.g., 叔父 = uncle)
   - Honorific title/rank (學生 = student/commoner rank)

4. **Print specifications**:
   - 위패 tablets are physical prints at mm-scale
   - 축문 prayer text is A4 size (210×297mm)
   - Both have specific font families and sizes defined
